Make You Blink

Keep your eyes open. Beat your best.

Make You Blink turns your iPhone into an arcade-style staring contest. Choose Timer Game or Rounds, keep your face centered with both eyes visible, and see how long you can last before a blink ends the challenge.

Face tracking happens live on your iPhone. No account is required, and your personal scores remain on your device.

Available after release

Why You'll Love It

  • Take on open-ended Timer Game challenges.
  • Advance through increasingly difficult rounds with three lives.
  • Track your best Timer and Rounds results on private leaderboards.
  • Get instant visual, sound, and haptic feedback when the game ends.
  • No accounts, ads, subscriptions, analytics, or tracking.

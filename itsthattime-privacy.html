It’s That Time: Car Care Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Overview

It’s That Time: Car Care helps you maintain a private record of your vehicles, completed service, upcoming maintenance, mileage, registration, inspections, and driver’s license renewals. This Privacy Policy explains what the app stores, which Apple services it uses, and the choices available to you.

It’s That Time has no company-operated account system or data server. TCQ Consulting LLC does not collect, view, sell, or have access to the vehicle records, photos, receipts, or renewal information you save in the app.

Information You Choose to Store

The app may store information you enter or select, including:

  • vehicle year, make, model, nickname, mileage, VIN, license plate, state, purchase date, and archive status;
  • service dates, mileage, services performed, shop or location, total cost, notes, and service receipts;
  • maintenance reminders, service intervals, warning preferences, registration dates, and state inspection dates;
  • driver names, license states, license expiration dates, and reminder preferences; and
  • vehicle photos and receipt images you intentionally select.

You are not required to enter optional information such as a VIN, license plate, shop, cost, notes, photo, or receipt to use the app’s core features.

Local Storage and iCloud Sync

Your app data is stored on your device. When iCloud is available for the app, Apple’s private CloudKit database may sync that data among your devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

CloudKit data is stored within Apple’s infrastructure and is governed by Apple’s privacy and security practices. It’s That Time does not operate a separate backend, and Timothy Connolly and TCQ Consulting LLC do not receive a readable copy of your private CloudKit records.

If iCloud is unavailable, the app may continue using local on-device storage. Whether data syncs or can be restored depends on your Apple Account, iCloud status, device settings, available storage, and Apple’s services.

Photos and Receipts

The app uses Apple’s system photo picker when you choose a vehicle photo or receipt image. The app receives only the image you select; it does not request unrestricted access to browse your entire photo library.

Selected images are stored with your app data on your device and may sync through your private iCloud database when iCloud sync is available. The current version does not request camera permission, record video, or capture audio.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, It’s That Time schedules notifications on your device for service dates, vehicle renewals, driver’s license renewals, and periodic mileage updates. Notification permission can be changed at any time in the app’s Settings screen or iPhone Settings.

Notification scheduling uses Apple’s notification services. TCQ Consulting LLC does not receive your reminder content or a history of your notifications.

External NHTSA Website

The app includes a link to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration website for recalls, manufacturer communications, and Technical Service Bulletins. Opening that link leaves the app. Any information you enter on the NHTSA website is provided directly to that website and is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. It’s That Time does not automatically send your VIN, license plate, or vehicle records to NHTSA.

No Analytics, Tracking, Advertising, or Data Sales

It’s That Time does not include third-party analytics, advertising networks, cross-app tracking, or an App Tracking Transparency request. The app contains no advertisements, and TCQ Consulting LLC does not sell or rent personal information.

Purchases

If you purchase It’s That Time through the App Store, Apple processes the transaction. TCQ Consulting LLC does not receive or store your payment card information. App Store purchases are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your records remain until you edit or delete them, delete the associated vehicle, remove the app’s data, or they are otherwise removed through your iCloud settings. Archiving a vehicle does not delete it; archiving preserves its service history and reminders while removing it from the active garage.

The app provides controls to delete vehicles, service records, maintenance reminders, and driver’s license records. Deleting an item removes it from the app’s active data store and, when iCloud sync is operating, the deletion is expected to sync to your other devices.

Deleting the app removes its local data from that device, but information previously synced to iCloud may remain in your private iCloud storage and may return after a reinstall. You can manage iCloud data through Apple’s device and account settings.

Information You Share With Us Directly

If you contact us for support, we receive only the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use that information to respond to your request and do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Children’s Privacy

It’s That Time is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The Last Updated date above will identify the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its App Store information when appropriate.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to support@tcqconsulting.org.

PackNudge questions

How do I prepare a packing list?

Start with a ready-made pack or create your own for a trip, errand, or everyday outing. Add or remove items, check them off, and track the progress of upcoming packs.

How do packing reminders work?

If you allow notifications, PackNudge schedules reminders locally on your device before departure. Notification settings on your devices control how reminders appear.

Can I use PackNudge offline or restore my packs?

PackNudge works offline. When signed in to iCloud, it uses your personal iCloud Documents storage to keep and restore packs on compatible devices. TCQ cannot read your packs.

PackNudge

Remember what matters before you leave.

PackNudge makes it easy to prepare for vacations, business trips, weekends away, road trips, errands, and everyday outings. Start with a ready-made pack, customize every item, check your progress, and choose helpful reminders before it is time to leave.

View on the App Store

Why You'll Love It

  • Choose from helpful ready-made packs or create your own.
  • Add, remove, and check off items as you prepare.
  • Schedule gentle reminders before your departure.
  • Track several upcoming packs and their progress in one place.
  • Works offline with no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • Keeps your packs private on your devices and in your iCloud account.

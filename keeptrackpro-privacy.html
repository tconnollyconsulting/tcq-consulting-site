KeepTrack Pro: Life Organizer Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

Overview

KeepTrack Pro is designed with privacy as a foundational principle, not an afterthought.

Data Storage

All data you enter into KeepTrack Pro, including receipts, warranties, bills, vehicle information, and every other module, is stored locally on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, synced through your own personal iCloud account.

We do not collect, see, sell, or have access to your data under any circumstances. KeepTrack Pro has no servers of its own. Your information never passes through Timothy Connolly or TCQ Consulting LLC. It stays entirely between your device and Apple's iCloud infrastructure, governed by your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policies.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud, KeepTrack Pro uses Apple's CloudKit framework to sync your data across your own devices signed into the same Apple ID. This sync is private to your Apple ID. Timothy Connolly and TCQ Consulting LLC have no access to this data, and no copy of it exists outside your personal iCloud storage.

Camera and Photo Library

KeepTrack Pro lets you attach a photo to receipts, warranties, and other items by taking a new photo or choosing one from your photo library.

These photos are stored on your device and, if enabled, within your own iCloud account along with the rest of your KeepTrack Pro data. We do not access, upload, view, or share any photo you add. Like all your KeepTrack Pro data, your photos remain private to you.

In-App Purchases

KeepTrack Pro offers one optional one-time purchase ("Pro") that unlocks additional modules. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. No payment information is collected, stored, or seen by Timothy Connolly or TCQ Consulting LLC.

No Tracking, No Analytics, No Ads

KeepTrack Pro does not use analytics, advertising networks, or tracking of any kind.

Children's Privacy

KeepTrack Pro is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to support@tcqconsulting.org

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as KeepTrack Pro evolves. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

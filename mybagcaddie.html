My Bag Caddie questions

How does My Bag Caddie recommend a club?

Enter your personal club distances, then enter the shot distance and playing conditions. The app calculates a recommendation on your device using the distances and preferences you supplied.

What information can I keep in my bag?

Store club selections, personal carry distances, confidence settings, preferred distance units, and other golf preferences. You can also keep round scores and course names you choose to enter.

Is my golf information uploaded?

No. Club recommendations are calculated on your device. My Bag Caddie does not transmit your bag, scores, or recommendations to a company server. It is a one-time purchase processed by Apple.

My Bag Caddie

Know your distances. Swing with confidence.

My Bag Caddie helps you understand the distances you can expect from every club in your bag. Enter the shot distance and playing conditions to receive a quick recommendation built around your own personal yardages. Designed to be simple, private, and easy to use whether you are on the course, at the range, or practicing in a simulator.

PageKey questions

Does PageKey store generated passwords?

No. It generates passwords on your device and can copy a result to the clipboard. It stores preferences, recently used seed titles, and purchase entitlements, not generated passwords.

How does clipboard clearing work?

Choose a clipboard timer of 30, 60, or 90 seconds in Settings. PageKey also clears the clipboard when the app is backgrounded and uses Apple biometric frameworks to lock the app.

Are there subscriptions or optional purchases?

PageKey has no subscriptions. Optional in-app purchases include individual packs and the PageKey Complete bundle. Apple processes purchases; consult the App Store for current pricing.

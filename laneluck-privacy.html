LaneLuck Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 11, 2026

Overview

LaneLuck is built to work entirely on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information LaneLuck accesses, how it is used, and what LaneLuck does not do with your data.

Information LaneLuck Accesses

LaneLuck requests two permissions from iOS: Location and Motion & Fitness.

Location data is used only while you have an active drive running to measure distance, speed, and route heading so LaneLuck can estimate driving movement and commute timing.

Motion & Fitness data, including device acceleration, is used only to help detect driving movements during an active drive. Neither permission is used outside of an active or just-completed drive.

LaneLuck stores your drive information locally on your iPhone. It does not transmit your location, motion data, or drive history off your device.

Where Your Data Lives

Every completed drive, including timestamps, distance, speed, and estimated driving movements, is saved as a file stored locally in LaneLuck's app storage on your iPhone.

LaneLuck does not use a backend server. There are no accounts, sign-in requirements, analytics services, or cloud sync in this version of the app. Your drive history remains stored locally on your device.

What LaneLuck Does Not Do

LaneLuck does not use analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not share, sell, or transmit your location history, motion data, or drive history to any third party.

LaneLuck does not transmit any data off your device. The app contains no advertisements and uses no advertising tracking identifiers.

Your Control Over Your Data

You can review your full drive history at any time in the app. You can permanently delete it using Clear All Drives in Drive History or Settings.

Deleting your history removes it from your device immediately. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is no remaining copy on a company server.

You can revoke Location or Motion & Fitness access at any time in iOS Settings. Revoking either permission may prevent LaneLuck from tracking drives correctly.

Children's Privacy

LaneLuck is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future Changes

If a future version of LaneLuck adds iCloud sync, a backend, analytics, or another feature that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version is released.

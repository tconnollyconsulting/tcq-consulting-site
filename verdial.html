Verdial questions

What does Verdial help me prepare?

Keep brand colors, product information, images, captions, hashtags, plans, drafts, and post history together. Create images and multi-image projects in the same workspace.

Does Verdial automatically publish to social platforms?

No. Verdial prepares content and hands control to you. You choose the destination, select media, review the text, and decide whether to post.

Where is my creative workspace stored?

Content is stored on your device and may sync through your personal iCloud account. TCQ Consulting LLC does not operate a separate content server. Once you share work, the destination service handles it under its own policies.

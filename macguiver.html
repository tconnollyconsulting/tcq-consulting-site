MacGuiver

Turn what you have into what you need.

MacGuiver is an offline field-intelligence utility that matches ordinary materials with supported project blueprints and helps you identify the materials required for supported projects.

View on the App Store

Why You'll Love It

  • Match ordinary materials with supported project blueprints.
  • Identify the materials required for supported projects.
  • Search the bundled material reference directly on your device.
  • Use the app without an account, internet connection, cellular service, or iCloud.
  • Your searches and material entries stay on your device.
Version 1.0.0 • Requires iOS 17.6 or later

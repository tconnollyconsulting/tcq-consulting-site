MacGuiver questions

How does MacGuiver help with materials and projects?

Use Workbench to match ordinary materials with supported project blueprints, or Reverse Engineer to identify materials needed for a supported project. The bundled material reference is also searchable.

Does MacGuiver work without internet access?

Yes. Its core features and bundled reference work without Wi-Fi, cellular service, an account, or iCloud.

Are my searches saved or sent to TCQ?

Searches and material entries are processed locally for the current session and are not sent to TCQ Consulting LLC. The app stores onboarding status and its bundled reference database locally.

SnapStreak questions

How do I play SnapStreak?

Choose a location pack, set the difficulty and optional timer, then capture photos for the challenges. Streaks are part of gameplay; the app does not store calendar dates for games or streaks.

What happens to my scavenger-hunt photos?

Photos are processed on your device. A qualifying high-score game saves trophy photos in private app storage; a newer high score for that pack replaces them. Photos are not uploaded or saved to your Photos library.

Does SnapStreak need an account or location permission?

No. The app uses camera permission for challenges, but does not request location access or require an account. Scores and progress remain local, without app-managed cloud sync.

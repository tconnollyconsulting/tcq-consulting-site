Make You Blink Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Overview

Make You Blink is a staring-contest game that uses supported iPhone face-tracking hardware to detect blinks and confirm that the player's face and both eyes remain visible. This Privacy Policy explains what the app accesses, what it stores, and how that information is handled.

Camera and Face Tracking

Make You Blink requests access to the front-facing camera. The app requires this permission to run its onboarding camera check, calibrate eye position, detect blinks, and determine whether the player's face and both eyes remain in the play area.

Apple ARKit processes the live camera feed on the device and provides face-position and eye-blink measurements to the game. Make You Blink does not save photographs, video, camera frames, face geometry, facial tracking measurements, or biometric templates.

The project does not request microphone, location, contacts, photos, motion and fitness, health, Bluetooth, or tracking permission.

Camera and face-tracking information is processed live on your iPhone. Make You Blink does not upload or retain camera images or facial tracking data.

Information Stored on Your Device

Make You Blink stores the following information locally in the app's preferences on your iPhone:

  • onboarding completion and acknowledgment of the in-app safety notice;
  • sound, haptic, flutter-warning, and visual-effect preferences; and
  • personal gameplay records, including Timer scores, Rounds scores, round numbers, elapsed times, dates, and calculated best results.

Timer and Rounds leaderboards each retain up to 10 top results. When a qualifying new result is added after the list is full, the lowest-ranked result is removed. Other locally stored settings remain until they are changed, reset by iOS, or the app and its data are deleted.

Network Services and Accounts

The audited version of Make You Blink contains no app-operated backend connection or network request code. It does not require or provide user accounts, sign-in, authentication, or Game Center.

The app does not transmit personal leaderboards, gameplay results, preferences, camera images, or face-tracking measurements to TCQ Consulting LLC or to a third-party server.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Make You Blink does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It contains no advertisements, advertising SDKs, tracking technology, or App Tracking Transparency request. TCQ Consulting LLC does not use the app to profile users or sell personal information.

Third-Party Services and Software

The audited Xcode project contains no third-party SDKs, Swift packages, package dependencies, or embedded third-party frameworks. It uses Apple system frameworks, including SwiftUI, ARKit, AVFoundation, Core Haptics, Foundation, and UIKit, for the interface, face tracking, audio, haptics, app preferences, and system integration.

Cloud Sync, Backups, and Sharing

Make You Blink does not implement iCloud or other cloud synchronization, and it has no in-app feature for sharing scores or camera content.

Local app preferences and scores may be included in an iPhone backup managed by Apple, depending on your device and backup settings. Apple, not Make You Blink or TCQ Consulting LLC, controls that backup process.

Your Choices and Data Retention

You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings. Without camera permission, Make You Blink cannot run its face-tracked gameplay.

This version does not contain a separate control for clearing all leaderboard and preference data. You can remove the app's locally stored data by deleting Make You Blink from your iPhone. If you restore the app from an Apple-managed backup, locally stored data may also be restored.

Children's Privacy

Make You Blink does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's safety notice advises that children play only with permission and supervision from a parent or guardian.

Future Changes

If a future version adds accounts, sharing, cloud synchronization, a backend, analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs, or another feature that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version is released.

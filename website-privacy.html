Website Privacy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This page explains measurement and contact information on tcqconsulting.org, the website of TCQ Consulting LLC. Each app has a separate privacy policy describing its own data practices.

Website analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions. App-card clicks and App Store button clicks are recorded with the app name. The website analytics code is separate from the iPhone apps and does not give TCQ access to the records stored inside those apps.

Google Analytics can process browser and device information and use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on your browser and consent settings. Google and the website hosting provider process technical information needed to deliver and measure the website, such as network requests. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Email support

If you email support@tcqconsulting.org, TCQ receives the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send and uses them to respond. Please do not include passwords or unnecessary private app records.

App Store and other links

Opening an App Store link takes you to Apple. Apple and other destinations handle information under their own policies. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Your choices

You can manage cookies and website storage in your browser. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported browsers. App storage, permissions, and iCloud choices are described in each app’s policy.

Questions

Contact support@tcqconsulting.org with questions about this website.

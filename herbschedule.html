Herb Schedule questions

How do I organize garden care?

Add your herbs and vegetables with their plant details and growing locations, then use Smart Calendars, care schedules, reminders, and care history to organize your checks.

What is an Away Care plan?

Create a PDF on your device from selected plants, dates, and scheduled checks, then share it with someone helping with your garden. It is a read-only reference and does not add events to their calendar.

Does Herb Schedule use my location or weather?

No. Growing locations are choices you enter yourself. Care recommendations are prompts to check plants and soil. Garden records may sync through your personal iCloud account.

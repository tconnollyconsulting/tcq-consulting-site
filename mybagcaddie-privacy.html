My Bag Caddie Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

Overview

My Bag Caddie is designed with one principle above all others: your golf bag and your data belong to you. We never see them. Nobody does. Not even us.

Information We Do Not Collect

My Bag Caddie does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information.
  • We do not store your club distances, scores, or round history on any server.
  • We do not transmit any data to any server.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not have access to your bag, scores, or recommendations at any time.
All club recommendations are calculated entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or sent to a server.

How My Bag Caddie Works

All club recommendations happen entirely on your device. When you enter a shot distance and playing conditions, the recommendation is calculated locally using your device's processor, based on the club distances and preferences you have entered. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Data Stored on Your Device

My Bag Caddie stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your club selections and personal carry distances.
  • Your confidence setting for each club.
  • Your experience level, golf ball type, and preferred distance unit.
  • Your Safe Picks setting.
  • Any round scores and course names you choose to enter.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Purchases

My Bag Caddie is a one-time purchase. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. My Bag Caddie never sees or handles your payment information.

Children's Privacy

My Bag Caddie does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@tcqconsulting.org .

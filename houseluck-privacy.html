HouseLuck Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Overview

HouseLuck is an iPhone app developed by Timothy Connolly for TCQ Consulting LLC. This policy explains how HouseLuck handles saved property information, nearby searches, calculator inputs, and open house notes.

Information Saved on Your iPhone

HouseLuck saves property addresses and map coordinates, your priorities, open house notes and visit dates, and saved calculator inputs on your iPhone. This lets you compare properties and pick up where you left off.

You do not need an account. We do not upload your saved information to our own servers, and HouseLuck does not sync through iCloud. If you back up your iPhone, the backup may include your saved app data.

Your saved notes, priorities, and calculator inputs stay in the app on your iPhone. Map searches and external links share the information needed for those features.

Maps and Location

HouseLuck uses Apple Maps services to find addresses, display maps, and search for nearby places. To provide these results, HouseLuck sends your search text and the location being searched to Apple.

If you choose Use My Location, HouseLuck asks for permission to use your location to help find the property you are visiting. It does not continuously track your location in the background. You can enter an address without granting location permission and can change permission in iPhone Settings.

Apple handles this information under its own privacy policies.

External Websites and Directions

HouseLuck opens external services when you choose their links or buttons. Property-listing searches send the saved address to Google Search. Street View sends property coordinates to Google. Flood-map links send the address or coordinates to FEMA or its ArcGIS viewer. Directions send the selected locations to Apple Maps.

Other external links include rail and environmental information. The websites you visit handle information under their own privacy policies. HouseLuck does not send your open house notes or calculator inputs through these links.

Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

HouseLuck has no ads or third-party analytics tools. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved information stays on your iPhone until you change it, remove it, or delete HouseLuck.

Clear Saved Properties removes saved properties and their associated open house notes. It keeps calculator numbers and priorities. You can erase notes by deleting their text and remove priorities within My Priorities.

Deleting HouseLuck removes the app’s locally saved data. Offloading the app keeps that data so you can use it again if you reinstall. Any copies in device backups are managed through your backup settings.

Information You Share With Us Directly

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information to answer your question. Please include only the details we need to help you.

Changes to This Policy

If HouseLuck’s features or the way it handles information change, we will update this policy and the Last Updated date above.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or support, contact support@tcqconsulting.org.

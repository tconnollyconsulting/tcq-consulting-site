2TR: Rhythm 2 Tha Rhyme questions

What can I do with 2TR?

Explore words and rhymes, hear selected words spoken with Apple system speech, bookmark discoveries, and save lyric drafts in a private writing workspace.

Does 2TR send my lyrics or searches to a server?

No. Searches and rhyme paths are processed inside the app. Drafts, bookmarks, history, and preferences stay on your device. 2TR has no account service or in-app cloud synchronization.

Can I control explicit content?

Yes. Explicit dictionary and rhyme content is hidden by default. You can enable or disable it in Profile. The setting remains on your device; consult the App Store age rating before downloading.

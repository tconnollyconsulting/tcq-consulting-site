Sum Of None questions

What is the goal of Sum Of None?

Plan your moves to bring the entire number matrix to zero. Guided Practice introduces the rules, and Easy, Medium, and Hard puzzles let you choose a challenge.

Can I resume a puzzle or track scores?

The app automatically saves an unfinished game and keeps up to 50 personal leaderboard scores on your device. A Daily Matrix gives you another puzzle to explore.

Does Sum Of None have accounts or online leaderboards?

No. This version has no account system, Game Center, backend, or cloud synchronization. Saved games, preferences, and your personal leaderboard stay in local app storage.

Plate Crib Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Overview

Plate Crib is a cribbage game that lets you score the visible letters and numbers on a license plate, play classic cribbage against the CPU, and learn the game through Practice Mode. Plate Crib is designed to work privately on your device without an account or a Plate Crib server.

TCQ Consulting LLC does not collect license plate entries, identify drivers or vehicles, track your location, sell personal information, or operate a Plate Crib account or gameplay server.

License Plate Entries

Plate numbers and optional state selections that you enter in Plate Play are processed locally on your device to calculate a score. Plate Crib does not use an entry to search for or identify a driver, owner, vehicle, or location.

Complete plate entries are not saved in game history, uploaded to TCQ Consulting LLC, or shared with third parties.

Saved Games and Settings

Plate Crib stores ongoing Classic and Practice game progress locally on your device so you can continue later. This may include cards, scores, dealer state, game phase, and your Practice Style preference. Saved card games do not contain license plate entries.

Starting a new game replaces the saved game for that mode. Deleting Plate Crib removes its locally stored app data from that device.

No Accounts or Cloud Sync

Plate Crib does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization. Game information is not stored in a TCQ Consulting LLC database or synced through a Plate Crib service.

No Location, Camera, Contacts, or Vehicle Access

Plate Crib does not request access to your location, camera, photo library, contacts, or vehicle information. The optional state selector is information you choose manually and is used only as part of the on-screen Plate Play experience.

No Advertising, Tracking, or Third-Party Analytics

Plate Crib does not contain third-party advertising, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics SDKs. TCQ Consulting LLC does not sell or rent personal information.

Support Communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and description of the issue. Please do not include a real license plate number or information that identifies another person unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Children's Privacy

Plate Crib does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account, profile, name, email address, or location to play.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Plate Crib evolves or its features change. The current effective date will always appear at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to support@tcqconsulting.org.

Common support questions

Where do I check device compatibility?

Open the app’s App Store listing for the current supported devices, iOS requirement, and release information.

Where do I check purchases?

Apple processes App Store purchases. Check your Apple purchase history and make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with your purchase. For an app-specific unlock problem, email the app name and a description of what you see; never send payment-card details.

Will deleting the app delete my data?

Deleting an app removes local app data, but private iCloud data or Apple-managed backups may remain. Read that app’s privacy policy before deleting data or reinstalling.

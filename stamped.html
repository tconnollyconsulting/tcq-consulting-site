Stamped questions

What can I remember with Stamped?

Capture places you visit, meals and drinks you enjoy, and experiences you want to revisit in your private journal. You can include photos and organize memories into Chapters.

Can I keep my journal across devices?

Stamped stores memories on your device and offers optional private iCloud sync. You control camera, photo library, and iCloud access through iPhone settings.

Which app should I use for yearly reminders?

Remember This Day focuses on written memories with date-based and annual reminders. Stamped focuses on keeping a journal of places, meals, drinks, and experiences.

Privacy & Content Policy

2TR: Rhythm 2 Tha Rhyme

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Overview

2TR: Rhythm 2 Tha Rhyme is a hip-hop dictionary, pronunciation-powered rhyme explorer, and private creative writing workspace. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not collect, and how its explicit content controls work.

Your lyrics belong to you. 2TR does not operate an account system or company server and cannot access the drafts you create.

Information Stored on Your Device

2TR stores your lyric drafts, bookmarks, recently viewed words, writing preferences, onboarding completion, content settings, and regional discovery preference locally on your device.

The bundled dictionary and pronunciation library are app resources. Your searches and rhyme paths are processed inside the app and are not sent to TCQ Consulting LLC.

Accounts, Servers, and Cloud Sync

2TR does not require or provide a user account. It does not operate a company server and does not implement in-app cloud synchronization.

Local app data may be included in an Apple-managed device backup depending on your iPhone and backup settings. Apple controls that backup process.

Pronunciation and Audio

2TR uses Apple system speech technology to speak selected words. The app does not record your voice, request microphone access, create voiceprints, or upload audio to TCQ Consulting LLC.

The rhyme engine uses a bundled offline pronunciation library containing more than 135,000 pronunciation records.

Explicit Language and Mature Content

2TR may contain profanity, slang, reclaimed or derogatory language, and references to drugs, alcohol, violence, weapons, sexuality, or other mature subjects. This material is included for dictionary, linguistic, cultural, rhyme, and creative writing purposes. Its inclusion does not constitute endorsement of hateful, harmful, illegal, or violent conduct.

Explicit dictionary and rhyme content is hidden by default. You may choose to enable Explicit Content in Profile and may turn it off again at any time. Your setting remains on your device and is not transmitted to TCQ Consulting LLC.

Lyrics and drafts are written by the user and may contain language of the user's choosing. Because that writing remains local, 2TR does not review or moderate it. Users and parents or guardians should follow the age rating displayed on the App Store.

Sharing

When you choose an available Share control, 2TR presents Apple's system share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you initiate the action and choose a destination. Information sent through another app or service is then governed by that recipient's privacy practices.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

2TR does not include analytics, advertising networks, tracking technology, or third-party crash-reporting software. It contains no advertisements and does not sell or share personal information with advertisers.

Third-Party Services and Software

2TR uses Apple system frameworks for its interface, local data storage, audio playback, haptics, and system sharing. The app does not include third-party SDKs or embedded third-party frameworks.

Purchases

The purchase of 2TR is processed by Apple through the App Store. Timothy Connolly and TCQ Consulting LLC do not receive or store your payment-card information. 2TR contains no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Data Retention and Deletion

Drafts remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its data. Bookmarks, history, and preferences remain until changed, reset by iOS, or removed with the app. Apple-managed backups may restore local data according to your device settings.

Children's Privacy

2TR is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app may contain mature language and references, and its App Store age rating should be considered by users, parents, and guardians.

Information You Share With Us Directly

If you contact us for support, we receive only the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your inquiry and do not sell it.

Future Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The Last Updated date will identify the current policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy may be sent to support@tcqconsulting.org.

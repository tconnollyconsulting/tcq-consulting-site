Remember This Day questions

How do memory reminders work?

Write a memory and choose a reminder date. You can repeat important reminders every year. The app requests notification permission when you choose a reminder.

Where are my memories stored?

Memories are stored on your device and may sync through Apple CloudKit in your personal iCloud account. TCQ Consulting LLC cannot view those private records.

How is this different from Stamped?

Remember This Day focuses on written memories and date-based reminders, including annual repeats. Stamped focuses on journaling places, meals, drinks, and experiences you want to revisit.
